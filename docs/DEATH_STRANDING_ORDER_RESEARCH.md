## Controlled order 331 test

The user offered to complete order 331 and report its resulting LL or LLL rank. A stable before-delivery capture was made at `Analysis/Validation/order331-before-20261022T024214Z` under the game's private backup folder. It includes `manualsave14` (9:38:03 PM), `autosave2` (9:39:44 PM) and `autosave3` (9:41:18 PM), October 21 America/Chicago. Each original/copy was checked for stable length/time and matching SHA-256; copied inputs were decrypted and their account and decompressed MD5 verified.

In the latest before-copy, order 331's candidate record has zero at the two small fields at relative offsets 10/12 and the packed field at 88. Those values remain raw observations; their semantics are unverified. The earlier Chiral Bridge snapshot has the same order-331 candidate bytes.

The user confirmed the result was **LLL**. Stable copies of `autosave4` (9:43:31 PM) and `manualsave15` (9:44:25 PM America/Chicago) were captured under `Analysis/Validation/order331-after-20261022T0252046180310Z`. Both matched the account and decompressed checksum; original/copied SHA-256 remained unchanged after analysis.

In the final manual save, order 331's 64-bit field at relative offset **72** changes from **0 to 0x100**. The game RTTI enum maps `EDSRewardRank.Rank_SSSS` to 8, consistent with a rank-history bit `1 << 8`. All other 539 standard-order fields at offset 72 remain unchanged. Some earlier records contain combinations such as 0x120 (bits 5 and 8), supporting a history of earned grades rather than a single latest-grade integer; that history interpretation remains an inference.

The intermediate autosave has an increment at offset 10 but not at offset 12 or the LLL bit. It appears to capture delivery before the final evaluation is committed. A new completion's rank must therefore be checked from a save made after the complete results sequence, rather than assuming every delivery-triggered autosave already contains its final grade.

The final manual save has **40 standard orders with bit 8 set**, including 331 (39 beforehand). A private named report is `LLL-completed-orders.md` within that after-run folder, with machine-readable evidence in `order-rank-calibration.json`. The current game `Rank_SSS` enum is 7; **no bit-7 flags are set** among these 540 records, but LL still needs a known positive control before that mapping is declared validated. The initial report was read-only; the subsequent user-requested tracker now includes the dated partial LLL import described above.

Next: corroborate the 40 count and another LLL example in-game; obtain a known LL example if available; validate table headers/bounds, grade bitmasks, repeated deliveries and lower-rank reruns. Determine whether offsets 10/12 are distinct delivery/premium counts. Preserve unknown for unsupported or ambiguous fields before enabling native order refresh.
